# Code of Conduct

Symbiote AGUI welcomes participants with different backgrounds, identities, abilities, and levels of experience. Everyone should be able to ask questions, contribute, and disagree without being harassed or demeaned.

## Expected behavior

- Treat other participants with respect and assume questions are asked in good faith.
- Discuss ideas and technical decisions without attacking the people behind them.
- Give specific, constructive feedback and accept that others may reach different conclusions.
- Respect privacy, credit others' work, and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Follow reasonable requests from maintainers to keep discussions relevant and productive.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal insults.
- Sexual harassment or unwelcome sexual content or attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Repeatedly disrupting discussions, targeting participants, or retaliating against someone who reports a concern.

## Scope

This code applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-managed spaces. It also applies when someone represents the project in public.

## Report a concern

Contact the maintainers privately at [team@rnd-pro.com](mailto:team@rnd-pro.com). Include what happened, relevant links or dates, and any context you can safely share. You may report behavior affecting you or another participant.

Do not publish private reports in issues or pull requests. Maintainers should handle reports discreetly and share identifying details only when needed to assess or address a concern.

## Maintainer response

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, ask participants to change their behavior, restrict participation temporarily, or exclude someone from project spaces. The response should reflect the severity, impact, and any repeated behavior. Serious misconduct may warrant immediate restrictions.

Participants are expected to respect these decisions and avoid retaliation. Concerns about a decision can be sent privately to the same contact address for reconsideration.
